# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People on macOS who need a private, on-device way to record a meeting's
microphone and system audio, then review a Hebrew, English, or mixed-language
transcript. They often open controls immediately before recording, when they
need confidence rather than configuration friction.

## Product Purpose

Quill makes local meeting capture and transcription deliberate, legible, and
safe. The controls surface should make the recording state, selected language
and engine, and output destination obvious without compromising the
menu-bar-first workflow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, reassuring. A compact native Mac utility with enough character
to feel cared for, not a generic settings pane or a copy of another app.

## Anti-references

- Dense, undifferentiated preference sheets with every option carrying equal
  visual weight.
- Decorative neon, glass effects, or generic AI-dashboard styling.
- A visual clone of Tamlil or any other transcription product.
- Interfaces that hide recording state or make consent-relevant choices feel
  incidental.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next consequential action unmistakable: start, stop, or review.
2. Treat local/private capture as a confidence signal, not marketing copy.
3. Group choices by the user's mental model—language, transcript, and files.
4. Preserve native macOS affordances, keyboard navigation, and accessibility.
5. Support Hebrew and English content naturally without forcing a single text
   direction onto controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use system semantic colors and controls, preserve keyboard focus order and
standard keyboard shortcuts, meet macOS high-contrast expectations, and avoid
motion that is not essential to state. UI labels remain localizable; Hebrew
transcript content must render right-to-left while the control surface keeps a
predictable system-language layout.
