# Privacy and consent

Quill is a local recording tool. It captures two files during a session:

- the default microphone input; and
- the Mac's system audio output, which can include calls, music, notification
  sounds, and other apps' audio.

Transcription runs locally. The optional Hebrew setup downloads software and
model weights once from Homebrew, PyPI, and Hugging Face; it does not upload
recorded audio.

You are responsible for obtaining consent and following the laws, contracts,
employer policies, and platform terms that apply where you record. Do not use
Quill to secretly record people or sensitive calls. A public fork or release
should retain this disclosure and should not claim legal compliance for every
country or use case.
